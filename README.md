# UGC Pokemon Card Grading Website

## Project Overview

The UGC Pokemon Card Grading Website is designed to provide users with an easy and efficient way to evaluate and grade Pokemon cards. With the increasing popularity of trading card games, collectors and players need a reliable platform to assess the condition and value of their cards.

### Key Features:
- **User-friendly Interface:** Intuitive design for easy navigation.
- **Real-time Grading:** Instant grading based on user inputs and card details.
- **Community Feedback:** Users can review and discuss grading results.
- **Educational Resources:** Guides and tips on card grading standards and practices.

### Purpose:
The purpose of this project is to create a community-driven platform that not only helps users grade their Pokemon cards but also fosters a passion for collecting and trading among enthusiasts. 

### Getting Started:
To get started, simply create an account, upload images of your cards, and let our grading algorithm assist you in evaluating your collection!
